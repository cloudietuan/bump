The barrier

Discovery was never the hard part.

Volleyball needs six people a side, a net, a reserved gym, and a group that already exists. Existing apps work well once you already play. “Intermediate 6v6, Thursday, 8pm” tells a beginner nothing they can act on.

“Am I intermediate?” is unanswerable if you've never played.

You set your level by describing what you can actually do, not by picking a number off a list.

Listings hide whether you'd be the worst player there.

Every game shows how many people going marked themselves new.

Walking into an unfamiliar gym alone is the real fear.

Each game carries a minute-by-minute schedule of what happens from the moment you arrive.

Nobody wants to get picked last.

Hosts assign the teams, and the listing says so before you commit.

For hosts

Anyone with a court reservation is supply.

Hosting takes a minute. Welcoming beginners is the default, and it comes with something you actually agree to rather than a badge you switch on.

  • Post a game in under a minute, from a court you've already booked.
  • Fill empty spots with people who are glad to be there.
  • Say something to newcomers in your own words, on the listing.
  • Know who's new before they walk in, so you can find them at the door.
