Six positions, plainly.

You don't have to pick one. Hosts put people where the team is short, and “I'll go wherever” is a normal answer.

OH Outside hitter Hits from the left and passes serve. New? A good first hitting spot.
OPP Opposite Hits from the right, blocks their best hitter. New? Less passing pressure than outside, but you hit from your off-hand side, which takes a while to feel normal.
MB Middle blocker Blocks everywhere, hits quick sets. New? The hardest spot to start in.
S Setter Takes the second ball and decides who hits. New? The highest-responsibility spot on the court.
L Libero Back-row defender in a different jersey. New? Uncommon in pickup.
DS Defensive specialist Back-row defender using a normal sub. New? A very common way to get court time while you're still learning to hit.
