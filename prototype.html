Bump

Volleyball is easy to love and hard to walk into.

Most apps help you find a game. Bump helps you find a game where nobody minds that you just started.

The barrier
You need six people, a net, a gym, and the nerve to show up alone.
The fix
Plain-language skill levels, first-timer counts, and a schedule of what happens when you arrive.
